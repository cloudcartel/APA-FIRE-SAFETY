Dasar Privasi & Terma

Bahasa Malaysia

Data yang dikumpul: nama syarikat dan pengurus, no. telefon, emel, maklumat bangunan, rekod alat pemadam api (pemeriksaan, servis, foto) dan log akaun.

Tujuan: mengurus akaun, rekod aset, peringatan servis dan laporan.

Pendedahan: kepada penyedia hosting (Supabase) yang mungkin menyimpan data di luar Malaysia, serta juruteknik atau rakan servis yang ditugaskan. Data tidak dijual kepada pihak ketiga.

Hak anda: mengakses, membetulkan atau meminta pemadaman data, dan menarik balik persetujuan, dengan menghubungi [EMEL PERHUBUNGAN].

Penyimpanan: selagi akaun aktif atau seperti dikehendaki undang-undang.

Terma: Rekod dalam APA CARE ialah rekod dalaman dan bukan sijil atau kelulusan rasmi JBPM/eFEIS. Kerja servis terkawal hanya boleh dilakukan oleh pihak yang layak. Pengguna bertanggungjawab ke atas ketepatan data dan kerahsiaan kata laluan.

English

Data collected: company and contact names, phone, email, building details, fire extinguisher records (inspections, service, photos) and account logs.

Purpose: account management, asset records, service reminders and reports.

Disclosure: to our hosting provider (Supabase), which may store data outside Malaysia, and to assigned technicians or service partners. Data is not sold to third parties.

Your rights: access, correct or request deletion of your data, and withdraw consent, by contacting [EMEL PERHUBUNGAN].

Retention: while your account is active or as required by law.

Terms: Records in APA CARE are internal records, not official JBPM/eFEIS certificates or approvals. Regulated service work must be done by qualified parties. Users are responsible for data accuracy and password confidentiality.
